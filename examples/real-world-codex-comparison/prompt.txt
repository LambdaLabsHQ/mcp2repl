REAL_WORLD_MODE_INSTRUCTIONS

Task:

You are helping an ordinary person choose a MacBook from the public Apple website. They want a laptop for remote work, many browser tabs, video calls, light photo editing, and occasional travel. They prefer portability, but they want the machine to last several years.

Hard constraints:

- Do not log in.
- Do not add anything to bag/cart.
- Do not start checkout.
- Do not enter personal information.
- Use public Apple web pages only.

Research the US English public Apple pages and interact with configurator / compare controls when useful. Keep the browser on `www.apple.com`, set or preserve US English locale when a prompt appears, and do not use non-US localized Apple pages. Use visible standard-consumer US upfront prices such as `$1,099`; ignore monthly installment prices such as `$91.58/mo` and education-savings ribbon prices.

1. https://www.apple.com/macbook-air/
2. https://www.apple.com/macbook-pro/
3. https://www.apple.com/mac/compare/
4. https://www.apple.com/us/shop/buy-mac/macbook-air
5. https://www.apple.com/us/shop/buy-mac/macbook-pro

Important browser-data budget:

- Do not dump full page text, full snapshots, full footers, or long legal sections into the model context.
- Prefer `evaluate_script` snippets that return only concise structured fields.
- Keep each browser extraction result under about 2,000 characters.
- Ignore footer navigation, legal fine print, unrelated accessories, and account/checkout links unless directly needed for a requested field.
- If a page is too long, extract headings, visible prices, and nearby sentences matching MacBook, Air, Pro, 13-inch, 14-inch, 15-inch, M-series chip, memory, storage, display, battery, ports, weight.

Compare at least these three shopping scenarios:

1. Portable value option: 13-inch MacBook Air with at least 16GB memory and at least 512GB storage.
2. Larger-screen Air option: 15-inch MacBook Air with at least 16GB memory and at least 512GB storage.
3. Higher-headroom option: 14-inch MacBook Pro with at least 16GB memory and at least 512GB storage.

For each scenario, extract from visible public pages:

- scenario
- productName
- officialUrl
- visibleStartingPrice
- configuredOrRelevantPrice
- chip
- memory
- storage
- display
- weightOrPortability
- batteryOrPowerClaim
- portsOrExternalDisplayNotes
- upgradeTradeoffs
- whyGoodForUser
- whyRiskyOrOverkill
- evidence: 2-4 short facts visible on the page

Return one compact JSON object with:

- userNeed
- options: array with at least the three scenarios above
- cheapestViableOption
- bestBalancedOption
- bestLongTermOption
- rejectedOptionsOrCaveats
- buyingChecklist: short list of what to verify before purchasing
- sources: final Apple URLs visited
- invariantPassed: true only if all three scenarios were researched, no login/cart/checkout was performed, every option has a productName, officialUrl, visible or configured price, chip, memory, storage, and at least two evidence facts, and none of those required fields are invented

Rules:

- Use browser interaction only through the allowed Chrome/browser automation path.
- Do not use web search unless an Apple page fails to load or clearly redirects to a new official Apple location.
- Do not use curl, wget, Python requests, direct HTTP clients, or filesystem scraping.
- Do not invent current prices or specs. Use "unknown" when the page does not expose a fact.
- For price fields, use the full upfront standard-consumer US dollar price from the shop size selector or configured buy URL, not the monthly installment amount and not an education-savings ribbon amount.
- For Pro memory/storage, use short capacity controls or specs such as `16GB` unified memory and `512GB` or `1TB` storage; never use testing, preproduction, battery-test, legal, or footnote lines as capacity evidence.
- Return only compact JSON.
